## Summary

A visitor lands in their own language when the site offers it, and can switch to any other from any
page.

## Why

The site is offered in 15 locales (NFR-I2) and each has its own address (NFR-I8). Delivers NFR-I5 and
NFR-I8. A link opens in the locale it was made in, so a shared run reads the way its player saw it,
and the visitor can switch.
