## Summary

Everything the site says around the kits and gear is in the visitor's language, with numbers, dates
and plurals as that language writes them, and a translation nobody fluent has checked says so.

## Why

Delivers NFR-I2, NFR-I4, NFR-I6 and NFR-I7. Fifteen locales will not each have a fluent reviewer at
launch, so the honest thing is to say which ones are unreviewed, on the site and in the README,
rather than let a machine translation pass as a checked one.
