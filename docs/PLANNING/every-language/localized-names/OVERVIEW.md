## Summary

Kits and gear carry their official names in every locale the game ships in, and the English names
in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Why

A player recognises a weapon by the name their game shows. Delivers NFR-I3. The names come from the
generated catalogue only, never from our own translation (`docs/RULES.md`).
